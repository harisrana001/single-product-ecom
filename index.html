How To Use Our Complete Bike Cleaning Kit

Follow our 3-step pro detailing sequence to get your motorcycle showroom-clean in under 20 minutes.

1. Pre-Rinse Road Grime
Rinse down the bike with low-pressure water to loosen surface dirt, road dust, and grit.
2. Spray Cleaner & Agitate
Spray Nano Bike Wash liberally, apply Degreaser to the chain, and agitate with the grunge brush.
3. Shield & Chain Lube
Rinse clean, microfiber dry, buff Ceramic Protect onto fairings, and apply ceramic chain lube.
